Delete your In/Out account

Request removal of your In/Out account and eligible account-associated data. You do not need to reinstall the app. A request is reviewed and fulfilled by IMR Tech SpA; submitting it does not mean deletion is already complete.

What happens to your data

We verify ownership before processing account information, support correspondence or optional welcome-email records. We explain any records retained for security, disputes or legal obligations in our response. Apple and Google retain their own purchase records. RevenueCat and infrastructure logs/backups follow their respective retention rules. Account deletion does not cancel subscriptions or erase copies already shared with others.

Delete data on your phone

In/Out → Settings → Local data → Delete local history removes sessions and Challenge test results, including their State Shift entries. It retains settings, saved routines, program progress and badges. Delete all local data also clears your local profile, preferences and saved content and cancels reminders. Revoke links first through Settings → Shared links. A website cannot remotely erase local app data.

Account availability

The current mobile build supports guest practice and does not yet create accounts. Production account support is planned. If you have a provisioned In/Out account, verify it below. Otherwise use the support request below; do not create a new account just to request deletion. In the app, Settings → Account & data requests opens this page.

Verify an existing account

We send a one-use sign-in link to your account email. After verification, review the scope and explicitly confirm your request. No deletion happens merely by opening a link.

No account or unable to verify?

Send a deletion request to davidclerc@imrtech.xyz from the address you used with In/Out. Tell us the data you want removed. IMR Tech SpA will verify ownership and explain the outcome or any necessary retention. Do not send your password. This route does not require the app.
