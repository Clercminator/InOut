Privacy policy

Developed and operated by IMR Tech SpA. Effective 2026-10-08 · Last updated 2026-10-08.

In/Out and this policy

In/Out is developed and operated by IMR Tech SpA. It is a wellness product, not a medical device, and is not intended to diagnose or treat medical conditions. This policy covers the mobile app and inout.imrtech.xyz.

Practice data on your phone

Sessions, Challenge attempts and personal bests, State Shift ratings, reported effects, program progress, badges, routines, reminders, preferences and optional profile details are stored locally. An optional profile photo is copied into the app’s private files. These are self-reported wellness records, not biometric measurements. They are not uploaded to our servers or sent to advertising or analytics providers. Guest practice works without an account.

Accounts and launch scope

Account support is planned for production. The current mobile build does not yet offer account creation or cloud history sync. Where an In/Out account has been provisioned, Supabase Auth processes its email, authentication credentials, user identifier and security/session information. The website can verify an existing account for privacy requests; it does not create an account merely because someone requests deletion. Account availability does not mean local history is backed up.

Subscriptions

Configured native purchases use Apple or Google billing and RevenueCat to check purchases, restore access and manage subscription entitlements. These services receive purchase and subscription records, an app-generated customer identifier, and device/app/network information necessary to operate billing. In/Out does not receive payment-card details. Purchase and entitlement information is required when you use these features. Subscriptions are optional.

Advertising and consent

The Free version includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google’s consent system. Live advertising requires production configuration and regional consent handling. Google may process IP-derived approximate location, device and advertising identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostics for delivery, measurement and fraud prevention, including with non-personalized ads. Practice ratings and history are not provided to Google. Pro suppresses ad requests. Review available choices in Settings → Plan & privacy → Ad privacy options. The current development setup uses test ads; final live-ad configuration requires validation before launch.

Optional sharing

Sharing an exercise uploads only its cadence and any nostril instructions to Supabase. A random public link grants access for 30 days unless revoked earlier. Names, ratings and notes are excluded. Keep its revocation control on your phone. Result and milestone sharing sends only the content shown in the sharing preview to your selected destination; the recipient or service can keep a copy. No public user profile or account-ID lookup exposes your history.

Optional welcome email

When enabled and explicitly requested, the welcome service sends your email, first name, selected goal, language and installation identifier to Supabase and Resend for one welcome message. It is not a marketing subscription. Retry payloads are removed on success or after the 23-hour retry window is reviewed; a salted email hash and delivery status remain for deduplication. Daily hashed installation/network counters limit abuse. The service is subject to owner configuration; disabling local retries cannot recall a sent message. Contact support to request deletion of server-side email records.

Support and privacy requests

Email support receives the address and message you send. Verified online privacy requests store your account identifier, email, requested scope, status and timestamps privately in Supabase so IMR Tech SpA can process the request. Only an authenticated, email-verified account can submit through that flow. Guest users and people unable to sign in can request assistance by email. We verify ownership before disclosing or deleting protected data. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or unnecessary wellness history.

Infrastructure, analytics and review access

Vercel hosts this website; Supabase hosts sharing, privacy-request and internal app-review services. They process ordinary IP/network and security information. This website adds no advertising, marketing pixels, analytics or tracking cookies. Verification tokens are used only for the active request and are not saved in browser storage. The app has no remote product-analytics or crash-reporting SDK; its event-name logger runs only in development. Apple and Google may independently handle store/device diagnostics. Internal reviewer access uses hashed installation/grant identifiers, expiry and revocation status; review credentials are never published here.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you remove them. Settings → Local data → Delete local history removes session history and Challenge test results while retaining routines, preferences, program progress and badges. Delete all local data removes the local profile, history, preferences and saved content and cancels app reminders. Revoke exercise links in Settings → Shared links before resetting. The website cannot erase a phone’s local storage. Cloud privacy requests are requests for processing, not a claim of immediate deletion. We retain request/support records only as needed to verify and fulfill requests, prevent abuse, resolve disputes or satisfy applicable obligations. We explain any applicable exception when responding; there is no universal fixed retention period established yet. Store transaction records are controlled independently by Apple/Google, and processor logs/backups may persist under their retention rules. Deletion does not cancel a subscription or erase copies shared with others.

Security and international processing

We use HTTPS for configured network services, restricted server access and ownership verification for privacy requests. Local data is protected by the app sandbox but has no separate database encryption. No system offers absolute security. Device backups and transfers are controlled by your operating system and can retain copies. Providers may process information outside your country; privacy questions and requests can be directed to IMR Tech SpA through Support.

Children, choices and changes

In/Out is not directed at children. Do not provide a child’s personal information through support or account forms. Contact us if you believe such information was provided. Optional photos and reminders use the system picker/notification permission; the app does not request camera, microphone, contacts, location or health-sensor access. We update this policy as practices change and show the effective and last-updated dates here. Account and advertising launch changes require a corresponding policy and store-disclosure review.
