Privacy policy

Effective 2026-09-29 · David Clerc

Development build and launch scope

This policy describes a development build preparing commercial v1. RevenueCat subscription and Google AdMob consent/banner adapters are included. Without store configuration, billing is unavailable in release builds and simulated with no charge in development. Native ads default to Google test units and load only after an explicit test-ad action. Live inventory requires the UMP privacy flow. These are not production integration acceptance. Final Privacy and Subscription Terms require review before launch.

Your practice data

IN/OUT stores your session history, optional tension ratings and reported effects, custom patterns, mixes, favorites, rituals, reminder schedules, profile details and settings on this phone. A profile photo you choose is copied into the app's local files. No account is required. State Shift and practice insights describe your own reports, not biometric or sensor measurements.

Collection and sharing

Practice history and tension ratings remain local and are not passed to advertising or analytics. With configured store keys, RevenueCat and the app stores process purchase, entitlement and app-user identifier information to provide subscriptions and restoration. When you review ad privacy choices and load a native test ad, Google consent/ad services receive the requests needed to provide those services. Production ad configuration is not enabled by default. The developer event logger accepts event names only, has no remote analytics sink and runs only in development. Audio and fonts are bundled. When you choose Share result, the shown text is passed to the service you select; that service receives the shared copy. Exercise sharing uploads breathing timings and any nostril instructions to a Supabase-hosted database, without names, ratings or notes. Anyone with the short link can open it until it expires after 30 days or is revoked by its creator. Revocation removes the stored cadence and prevents future loads; it cannot erase copies already downloaded. A separate revocation secret stays on the creator's phone. Sharing services receive ordinary network request information. The browser exercise stores no history and sends no analytics.

Device backups and security

Your phone's operating system may include app data in backups or device transfers, depending on your settings. Those copies are managed by your platform or backup provider. Data is stored within the app's sandbox; IN/OUT does not provide separate database encryption or cloud recovery. Anyone with access to your unlocked phone may be able to open your history.

Retention and deletion

Local data stays in the app until you delete it or remove the app's data. Delete individual sessions in History and individual routines in your saved library. Settings includes Delete local history and Delete all local data. Deletion in IN/OUT does not remove copies you previously shared or copies held in device backups. Manage those through their respective services. Delete reminders on the Reminders screen. Delete all local data also cancels reminders scheduled by IN/OUT. Revoke hosted exercise links in Shared links before deleting app data. Deleting local data does not revoke remote links; they remain accessible until expiry and their local revocation controls are lost. Expired link records are removed during service cleanup; provider backups may retain earlier copies under their retention policies.

Support and platform services

If you contact support, we receive what you choose to send, such as your email address and issue description. Do not include medical information or tension ratings unless necessary for your request. Support correspondence is used to respond to you and resolve the issue, and retained only as needed for that purpose or applicable obligations. App stores and operating systems may separately process installation, download, or diagnostic information under their own policies.

Permissions and changes

IN/OUT does not request microphone, camera, location, contacts or health-sensor access. Choosing an optional profile photo opens your phone's photo picker. Enabling a reminder requests notification permission; delivery is managed by your operating system and may be delayed. Notifications use a neutral message without your profile name, ritual name or ratings. Audio, vibration and screen-awake behavior are controlled in Settings. We update this policy when our data practices change.

Public privacy and support website

The public privacy and support pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. Opening them sends ordinary web requests, which may include your IP address and browser information, to that hosting provider. These pages use no IN/OUT analytics, cookies or advertising. The privacy and help text bundled inside the app can be read offline without opening the website.
