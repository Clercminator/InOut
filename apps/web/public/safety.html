Breathing safety

Effective 2026-09-16 · David Clerc

Wellbeing, not medical care

IN/OUT is a breathing practice tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition. Results are not guaranteed. Ask a qualified healthcare professional if you have concerns about whether a breathing practice is appropriate for you.

Keep it comfortable

Practice seated or lying somewhere safe. Breathe gently without forcing depth or speed. Holds are optional: return to natural breathing whenever you need to. The timer is a guide, not a target to push through.

Stop when you need to

If you feel dizzy, faint or unwell, use I feel unwell to pause the guide immediately, then end the session and breathe naturally. Seek medical help for severe or persistent symptoms. The app is not an emergency service.

Choose a safe place

Do not follow breathing exercises while driving, operating machinery, swimming, bathing, or in or near water. Do not use breath holds as a competition. High-intensity cyclic breathing is not included in this release.
