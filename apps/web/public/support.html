Subscriptions and restore

Open Settings → Plan & privacy → Pro & subscriptions, then Restore purchases. Use the same Apple or Google account used to buy Pro. Cancel renewal or request a refund through that store. Deleting local data does not cancel a subscription.

Accounts and data

Guest practice is available now; production account support is planned. The current mobile build stores history on your device and does not sync it to an account. Use the deletion pages for local controls, verified account requests and help when you cannot sign in.

A session paused or sound is missing

App switching, screen lock and supported interruptions pause the guide. Return and choose Resume, Restart or End session. Check Sound in Settings, device volume and headphones. Audio follows the iPhone silent switch. Haptic behavior depends on your device.

Offline use and moving phones

Breathing, saved routines, State Shift and history work offline. Billing, hosted shares, email and privacy requests need a connection. Local history is not cloud-backed by In/Out; device backups and transfers depend on your platform.

Safety

Stop if you feel dizzy or unwell. Breathe naturally in a safe seated or lying position. Never practice while driving or in or near water. In/Out is a wellness tool, not emergency or medical care.
