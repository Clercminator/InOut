Help & support

Effective 2026-09-16 · David Clerc

My session paused

App switching, screen lock and supported audio interruptions pause the guide. Return to the session and choose Resume, Restart or End session. After a forced closure, the app recovers the last saved checkpoint and waits for you to resume.

I cannot hear guidance

Choose Sound cues or Concise voice in Settings. Check your phone volume, headphones and the iPhone silent switch. Audio follows the silent switch. Haptics may be limited by your device or system settings.

Can I use IN/OUT offline?

Yes. Protocols, custom routines, sessions, State Shift and history work without a network connection. Sharing through another service and contacting support may require internet access.

Where are my routines?

Open Custom, then Saved Presets or Saved Mixes. Use Save routine in the editor to keep a routine on this phone. Editing or deleting a saved routine does not change previous sessions.

Moving phones or deleting data

This release has no IN/OUT account or cloud sync. Device backup and transfer behavior depends on your operating system. Removing app data may permanently remove your history and routines. Delete all local data in Settings resets the app, including an interrupted session.
