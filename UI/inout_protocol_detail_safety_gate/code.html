Protocol Telemetry & Science N=4 CLINICAL REF
01

Sit upright with an unconstrained spine. Ground feet flat on floor or sit cross-legged.

02

Exclusively inhale through the nose. Engage lower diaphragm, keeping shoulders locked downward.

03

Hold without clamping the glottis shut. Keep throat soft and intra-thoracic pressure stable.

Equalizing inhalation and exhalation regulates blood CO2 partial pressure while preventing hypercapnia. The intentional hold phases stimulate the baroreflex mechanism, engaging the parasympathetic brake via the vagus nerve.

Target HRV Impact +18% RMSSD shift

Developed and standardized within Tier-1 military units (US Navy SEALs) as an arousal-control protocol prior to high-threat tactical incursions. Documented by Mark Divine and validated in peer-reviewed psychophysiology literature for immediate salivary cortisol reduction.

Box breathing carries minimal neurological risk. If mild dizziness occurs, suspend the holds and return to uncontrolled tidal breathing. Never force breath retention beyond your functional air hunger threshold.
