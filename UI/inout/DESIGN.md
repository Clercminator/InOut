## Brand & Style

This design system is built for elite conditioning, high-performance physical readiness, and functional breath mechanics. Rooted in an aesthetic intersection of human performance instrumentation (WHOOP, Garmin) and stark athletic typography (Nike Training, SmartWOD), the visual ethos strips away all holistic, pastel, and pseudo-spiritual wellness tropes. 

### Brand Personality & Philosophy
- **Ruthless Minimalism:** Pure utility over ornament. Space is carved with dark charcoal surfaces, hairline geometry, and authoritative typography. 
- **Athletic Precision:** The UI operates like a precision stopwatch or telemetry monitor: deliberate, uncompromising, and instantly readable during high-strain states.
- **Biometric Immersion:** Interactions respond with tight tactile cadence and intentional chromatic shifts keyed strictly to metabolic and neurological states.

### Design Movement
**Technical High-Contrast Minimalism paired with Tactile Biometric Modernism.** Elements employ deep optical layers of near-black basalt and obsidian tones, articulated hairline dividers, engineered pill meters, and focused luminescent accents reserved solely for states of respiration (Inhale, Hold, Exhale, Hold) and biological protocol goals.

## Colors

The palette uses a zero-saturation dark spectrum as its bedrock, ensuring protocol accents possess acute luminous efficiency without visual pollution.

### Base Canvas & Surfaces
- **Canvas Base:** `#08090C` (Absolute background; immersive, distraction-free substrate).
- **Surface Level 1 (Cards & Modules):** `#0B0D11` (Default structural layer).
- **Surface Level 2 (Elevated Panels):** `#14171F` (Interactable items, protocol selectors).
- **Surface Level 3 (Active / Raised Overlays):** `#1C202C` (Input fields, pressed states, sheet modals).
- **Hairline Borders:** `#242A38` (Precision boundaries with 1px thickness; crisp optical containment).

### Content & Typography
- **Primary Typography:** `#F8FAFC` (Slate White; maximum legibility, zero harsh optic glare).
- **Secondary Typography:** `#94A3B8` (Mid-tone slate; data labels, time units, secondary metrics).
- **Tertiary / Inactive Typography:** `#64748B` (Muted gray; unselected metrics, inactive protocol indicators).

### Functional Protocol Accent Tokens
Colors are never applied decoratively; they strictly map to neurological and respiratory objectives:
- **Focus (Electric Cobalt):** `#3B82F6` (Default core brand accent; box breathing, steady-state acuity).
- **Perform (Energetic Orange):** `#F97316` (Pre-workout hyperventilation, hypoxic tolerance, VO2 conditioning).
- **Energize (Bright Amber / Red-Orange):** `#F59E0B` (Quick stimulation, sympathetic activation, morning primer).
- **Calm (Electric Cyan / Teal):** `#06B6D4` (Parasympathetic regulation, down-regulation, anxiety triage).
- **Recover (Crisp Emerald):** `#10B981` (Post-strain recovery, CO2 tolerance testing, restorative cadences).
- **Sleep (Deep Violet / Indigo):** `#8B5CF6` (4-7-8 method, non-sleep deep rest, twilight transition).

## Typography

Typography prioritizes rapid recognition at high physical exertion.

### Hierarchy & Typesetting
- **Display & Large Numerics:** Driven by tabular, monospaced-flavored athletic glyphs. Numbers never shift width during breath count cycles.
- **Headlines & Text:** Standardized on `Inter` with tight negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.04em`) to mirror athletic editorial and cockpit HUD typography.
- **Technical Metas & Telemetry:** Driven by `Space Grotesk` in uppercase styling (`label-caps`) with deliberate wide tracking (`0.08em`), establishing visual tension against the compressed headlines.
- **Rule of Case:** All protocol statuses (INHALE, EXHALE, HOLD, REST) and diagnostic metrics (BPM, SEC, SET, ROUND) must be rendered in strict uppercase using `label-caps` or `label-mono`.

## Layout & Spacing

A mobile-first, performance-optimized viewport structure built for single-thumb control and glanceable status checks.

### Layout Mechanics
- **Mobile Foundation (< 768px):** Single-column vertical stream, pinned 16px (`1rem`) outer margins. Critical pacing indicators, countdown dials, and session triggers sit locked within the thumb zone (lower 40% of the screen).
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 6-column fluid grid, 24px margins. Allows two-up card splits for protocol diagnostics and real-time biometric metrics.
- **Desktop / Large Displays (> 1024px):** 12-column fixed grid with a max-width container of `1120px`. The active breath visualizer takes central focus with persistent modular telemetry rails left and right.

### Spatial Rhythm
- **Rhythm Multiplier:** 4px baseline grid. 
- **Component Density:** Dense internal card paddings (`space-md` to `space-lg`) bound tightly with razor borders ensure maximum usable surface area for visualizations and dynamic waveform trackers.

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally rejects muddy drop shadows and faux blur skeumorphism in favor of calibrated planar surfaces and ambient luminous radiometry.

### Surface Stratification
1. **Backdrop (0dp):** Deep Canvas `#08090C`. Zero elevation, absorbing ambient light.
2. **Structural Tier 1 (1dp):** `#0B0D11` bordered with a 1px solid hairline `#242A38`. Used for standard content sections, static metrics, and inactive workout tiles.
3. **Interactive Tier 2 (2dp):** `#14171F` bordered with `#242A38`. Used for active cards, button baselines, and protocol carousels. On hover or tap, the border transitions to a 30% alpha of the protocol's active accent color.
4. **Focused Overlay Tier 3 (3dp):** `#1C202C` bordered with `#3B82F6` (or current protocol accent). Reserved for active countdown wheels, sheets, and alert dialogues.

### Chromatic Glows & Field Radiance
- Instead of black shadows, high-intensity moments (e.g., peak breath hold, maximum lung capacity) utilize a radial blur wash: `box-shadow: 0px 0px 48px -8px [protocol-accent-hex]22` (15% opacity).
- Timer indicators maintain a focused, sharp, 1px optical ring rather than a thick, glowing aura, prioritizing clinical exactness over fantasy aesthetics.

## Shapes

The geometric personality balances industrial engineering with ergonomic tactile contact.

### Radii Rules
- **Base Cards & Major Containers:** Exactly `16px` (`rounded-lg`), delivering structural solidity while eliminating razor corners that distract from data.
- **Buttons, Modals & Form Modules:** Exactly `12px` (matching the intermediate scale), ensuring clean nesting inside 16px parent cards.
- **Status Pills, Protocol Tags, & Phase Markers:** Full pill curvature (`9999px`), distinguishing informational tags and active states immediately from layout structural containers.
- **Hairline Thickness:** Strictly `1px` everywhere. No heavy outline styles.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Session Start / Stop):** Height 56px. Background `#F8FAFC`, text `#08090C`, font `Inter` 16px Bold. Rounded 12px. Active press scales down slightly (`scale(0.98)`). No outline.
- **Protocol Action (Active Context):** Adopts the protocol's signature color (e.g., `#F97316` for Perform) with text set in stark `#08090C`.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Height 48px or 56px. Background `#14171F`, border 1px solid `#242A38`, text `#F8FAFC`. On hover/tap, border shifts to `#94A3B8`.

### Chips & Protocol Badges
- **Shape:** Full pill (`roundedness: 9999px`).
- **Inactive / Neutral:** Background `#14171F`, border 1px solid `#242A38`, text `#94A3B8`, uppercase `label-caps`.
- **Active Protocol Badge:** Background tinted at 15% opacity of the protocol color (e.g., `#06B6D426`), border 1px solid `#06B6D4`, text `#F8FAFC`. Contains an optional 6px solid circular pulse indicator.

### Breathing Engine Ring (Visualizer)
- **Geometry:** Concentric dual-ring vector. Outer static calibration ring in hairline `#242A38`. Inner kinetic ring rendered in the selected protocol color with a continuous stroke-dashoffset animation.
- **Phase Label:** Centered directly above the primary counter; strictly set in `label-caps` (e.g., `INHALE (4S)`).

### Metric & Habit Tracker Cards
- **Structure:** Background `#0B0D11`, 1px border `#242A38`, 16px corner radius.
- **Header:** Secondary slate label in `label-caps`, accompanied by an icon or micro-pill status badge on the right.
- **Metric Value:** Set in `Space Grotesk` tabular numerals (`headline-xl`).
- **Habit Matrix Grid (HabitKit-style):** 7-column or 30-day square grid. Empty days are filled with `#14171F`. Completed days fill with solid protocol accent, creating an immediate visual punch-card of consistency.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Input Text:** Background `#14171F`, 1px border `#242A38`, height 48px, rounded 12px. Text `#F8FAFC`, placeholder `#64748B`. Focused state introduces a crisp 1px border transition to `#3B82F6` without gross glow blooms.
- **Numeric Steppers (Duration / Reps):** Segmented buttons flanking a tabular numeric readout. Decrement/Increment triggers are 44x44px target tap areas in `#1C202C`.

### Lists & Row Items
- **Structure:** Divided by 1px hairline border `#242A38`. Padding `16px 0`.
- **States:** Hover/pressed surfaces transition to `#14171F`. Row starts with a monospaced session index (`01`, `02`) in `#64748B`, followed by protocol name in `#F8FAFC` and cadence structure (e.g., `4-4-4-4`) in `label-mono`.
